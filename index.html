Step 1 of 5

Cas9 carries a guide

Cas9 is a DNA-cutting enzyme. A guide RNA associates with it and can base-pair with a matching DNA region.

What the enzyme labels mean: Cas9 contains two nuclease domains, commonly called HNH and RuvC, that cut opposite DNA strands. The guide RNA is not an enzyme. After the cut, the cell’s own repair proteins handle the DNA break; different repair routes can produce different outcomes.
